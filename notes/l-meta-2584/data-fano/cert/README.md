# Certificates for the two closed Heawood cases (s = 26 gates, t = 12 and t = 13 tops)

What is certified: the CNF produced by the generator is unsatisfiable. What the CNF means (no
least TopForm circuit for the Fano incidence function with exactly 26 gates and t tops in the
block normal form) rests on the encoding argument in `../../README.md` (iteration 4) and on the
checked lemmas it cites; that part is not machine-checked.

Generate (from `tools/fano`, deterministic):

    python3 -I topsat4.py 26 12 14 $(cat heawood.txt) --extra --lemmaV 7 --exactX
    python3 -I topsat4.py 26 13 14 $(cat heawood.txt) --extra --lemmaV 7 --exactX

Each writes `blk4_s26_t{t}_n14.cnf` when the verdict is not SAT.

| t | vars | clauses | sha256 of the CNF | solve | LRAT lemmas |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 12,225 | 146,087 | c7279e40ac91190d2e1810222cd2f9478f1070cbedc14215250fc1ff5d05f54c | 14.6 s | 336,271 |
| 13 | 10,715 | 113,791 | 5606994ee6c67901a72afcd167f63528a57264fe707fba68e4b4fa05d9ebf8b7 | 0.4 s | 46,127 |

Proofs: CaDiCaL 2.1.2, `cadical -q --lrat=true --binary=false F.cnf F.lrat`. The t = 13 proof is
stored here gzipped; the t = 12 proof (114 MB, sha256 06903283155eefc99921425a0b0cd6f08f5d98488670d785a290a9e62da03ab6)
is regenerated by the same command.

Check: `python3 -I tools/fano/lratcheck.py F.cnf F.lrat`, a 70-line checker written for this
(RUP steps only, every hint must be unit or conflicting, the empty clause must be added). It
accepts both proofs and rejects three corrupted variants of the t = 13 proof (a hint dropped from
the last step, a literal flipped in the first lemma, the empty-clause step removed).
